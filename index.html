How Speakifly works

Every level, the same reliable pattern

No matter your starting point, each level follows a structure designed to build real spoken fluency, not just test knowledge.

1

Learn out loud

New words, sentences or grammar, each with a phonetic guide and a "Hear it" button so you always know how it should sound.

2

Speak it back

Every level ends with a speaking prompt. Use the built-in microphone practice to check your pronunciation against the target phrase.

3

Prove it in writing

Short fill-in-the-blank and multiple-choice questions lock in what you've learned before you move to the next level.
